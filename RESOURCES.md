# Resources

## Tools

- [Semgrep](https://semgrep.dev/), [CodeQL](https://codeql.github.com/), [Bandit](https://github.com/PyCQA/bandit) — scanning training data and completions.
- [gitleaks](https://github.com/gitleaks/gitleaks), [TruffleHog](https://github.com/trufflesecurity/trufflehog) — secret removal.
- [ScanCode Toolkit](https://github.com/aboutcode-org/scancode-toolkit) — licence and provenance detection.
- [datasketch](https://github.com/ekzhu/datasketch) — MinHash LSH near-deduplication.
- [Purple Llama / CyberSecEval](https://github.com/meta-llama/PurpleLlama) — insecure-code benchmarks.
- [BigCode project](https://www.bigcode-project.org/) — The Stack datasets and opt-out tooling.

- Hubinger et al., "Sleeper Agents: Training Deceptive LLMs that Persist Through Safety Training", 2024.
- Carlini et al., "Poisoning Web-Scale Training Datasets is Practical", IEEE S&P 2024.

- Pearce et al., "Asleep at the Keyboard? Assessing the Security of GitHub Copilot's Code Contributions", IEEE S&P 2022.
- He and Vechev, "Large Language Models for Code: Security Hardening and Adversarial Testing", ACM CCS 2023.
- Tran, Li and Madry, "Spectral Signatures in Backdoor Attacks", NeurIPS 2018.
- Chen et al., "Detecting Backdoor Attacks on Deep Neural Networks by Activation Clustering", 2018.
- Kocetkov et al., "The Stack", 2022; Lozhkov et al., "StarCoder 2 and The Stack v2", 2024.
- Lee et al., "Deduplicating Training Data Makes Language Models Better", ACL 2022.
